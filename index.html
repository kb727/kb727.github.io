About KB727

KB727 is a Shanghai-headquartered industrial investment and asset management platform. We acquire, redevelop and operate underutilized industrial parks, industrial land and logistics and warehouse properties, converting low-efficiency sites into modern industrial space for advanced manufacturing, innovation and supply-chain operations.

Team and Track Record: The core team brings an average of over 25 years of industry experience, with more than 80 completed asset transactions and RMB 10 billion+ in cumulative redeveloped asset value. It combines this experience with deep familiarity of China's regulatory framework and the handling of complex ownership structures.

Core Businesses: Industrial park revitalization — repositioning aging plants and vacant innovation campuses into modern industrial communities. Industrial land intensification — compliant redevelopment, vertical industrial use and mixed-use upgrading. Logistics and warehouse upgrade — transforming conventional distribution assets into modern supply-chain infrastructure.

Delivery Models: Three models, matched to asset stage and partnership terms — proprietary development (own capital to acquire, redevelop and operate), joint development (shared investment and risk with industrial partners or co-investors) and mandated development (investment judgment, redevelopment management and operating services on behalf of asset owners or investors).

Collaboration Network: Long-term working relationships with industrial investors, developers and operators, providing end-to-end resource coordination from project incubation to stabilized operation.

Value Proposition: For asset owners, we turn underused assets into tradable, operable and financeable properties; for industrial users, we deliver purpose-built space for R&D, manufacturing and logistics; for investment partners, we provide assets with clear fundamentals and stable operating performance.
