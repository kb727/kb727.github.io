About KB727 Group

KB727 Group is a professional logistics investment and acquisition service provider headquartered in Shanghai, China. As a trusted representative of major logistics capital for investment and acquisition, we leverage extensive global networks and synergistic multi-business models to build an integrated, end-to-end multimodal logistics ecosystem—fully digitized and intelligently interconnected across the entire value chain.

At the core of our strategic vision lies the "3+N" business matrix: port logistics investment, ocean freight services, and industry-specific logistics solutions—complemented by a dynamic portfolio of "N+" growth drivers, including air and land transportation. This approach fosters deep domain expertise, maximizes resource synergy, and elevates systemic operational capability.

With over 100 affiliated companies and nearly 6,000 employees worldwide, we continue advancing business specialization and cluster-based organizational development, proactively seizing opportunities presented by globalization.

Our Services

1. Port Logistics Investment

Investment and operation of port terminals, container yards, and inland port logistics centers. Integrated services including container handling, bulk cargo storage, and customs clearance.

2. Ocean Freight Services

Comprehensive ocean freight solutions covering FCL, LCL, bulk cargo, and special cargo transportation. Partnerships with major shipping lines for global coverage.

3. Industry-Specific Solutions

Tailored logistics solutions for manufacturing, automotive, electronics, pharmaceuticals, and retail industries. Dedicated warehousing and distribution services.

4. Logistics Asset Acquisition

Professional acquisition services for logistics real estate, warehouses, distribution centers, logistics parks, and port assets with thorough due diligence.

5. Multimodal Transport

Integrated multimodal transport combining sea, land, air, and rail transportation with seamless intermodal connections and intelligent tracking systems.

6. Logistics Technology

Investment in intelligent booking systems, dynamic ship monitoring, container tracking, and one-stop service platforms leveraging AI technologies.
